# R&D Internships 2027: Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS), AI & Signal Processing

The **SURFO team at SNCF Réseau** is offering **5 applied research internships** focused on **Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS)** and the analysis of vibrations measured using optical fibers.

- **Start date:** From March 2027
- **Duration:** 4 to 6 months
- **Target candidates:** Final-year engineering students and Master's (M2) students, particularly those specializing in signal processing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, or applied mathematics.
